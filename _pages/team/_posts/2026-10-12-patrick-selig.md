I did my Ph.D. at University College Dublin in collaboration with Equal1 Labs. My work focused on applying machine learning to challenges faced by Equal1 in the field of quantum computing. A specific focus was on quantum error mitigation and quantum architecture search.

As a computer scientist with a PhD, specializing in machine learning and data analysis, I develop ML solutions built on solid data analysis, from quantum computing and additive manufacturing to agricultural machinery. From data architecture and model training to evaluation, I work on projects in close collaboration with domain experts from industry and research, for example at Equal1 Labs and NEXAT. Finding a common language in interdisciplinary teams is part of that.
