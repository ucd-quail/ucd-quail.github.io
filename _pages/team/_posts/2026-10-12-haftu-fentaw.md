I am a Machine Learning Engineer and AI researcher specializing in Reinforcement Learning, Generative AI, and intelligent control systems. My research focuses on applying deep reinforcement learning to quantum control, with the goal of developing precise, robust, and adaptive control strategies for quantum systems. My broader interests include quantum control optimization, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), GraphRAG, computer vision, and scalable AI systems. Combining industry experience in designing and deploying production-grade AI solutions with academic research, I am passionate about bridging the gap between theoretical advances and practical applications to develop intelligent, reliable, and scalable systems.
