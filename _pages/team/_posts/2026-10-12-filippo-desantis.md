I am a PhD researcher at University College Dublin (UCD) School of Computer Science, funded by Rinn AI, where I explore the intersection of Quantum Computing, Artificial Intelligence and financial technology. My research focuses on developing Quantum AI techniques and investigating their potential applications in the fintech sector.

My academic journey began at Politecnico di Milano, where I earned a BSc in Mathematical Engineering, followed by an MSc in High Performance Computing Engineering. After graduating, I spent a year working as a software developer in the banking sector.

Driven by a strong enthusiasm for Quantum technologies and their transformative potential, I am now pursuing my passion for research, exploring how Quantum Computing and AI can open new possibilities for the future.
