On the 5th and 6th of November, Filippo took part in a Rinn AI kick-off event at the Hudson Bay Hotel in Athlone, welcoming the new cohort of PhD students for the 2026–2030 period.
It was an amazing opportunity to meet fellow PhD candidates from all over Ireland, each coming from diverse backgrounds and bringing different perspectives and contributions to AI research through their respective topics.

As part of the networking activities, he also had the opportunity to attend four lectures covering key topics such as AI ethics, science communication, and diversity and inclusion in the research environment. These are all fundamental aspects of the development of an early-stage researcher, particularly at the beginning of a PhD journey.

The event highlighted how technical skills need to be complemented and supported by soft skills to reach their full potential. These include effective communication with fellow researchers and the wider public, fostering a positive and inclusive research environment, and ensuring that scientific discoveries and research methods are guided by strong ethical principles.
